# 06. Subscription and Billing Requirements

---

## 1. Business model

- **Players** never pay.
- **Organisations** pay a recurring subscription, monthly or yearly. The subscription belongs to the organisation. Any approved manager of the organisation can buy and manage it.
- Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). Offload is not VAT-registered, so no VAT is charged and the price shown is the price paid.
- Card payments, including Apple Pay and Google Pay, are processed by Stripe, the provider already used by Offload. Card details are never stored by Offload.

**SUB-1.1** THE Pricing page SHALL show all plans for every organisation type, with a monthly / annual switch, to visitors and signed-in users.

**SUB-1.2** THE System SHALL let a manager buy only the plans for their organisation's type. Plans for other types are shown but cannot be bought.

**SUB-1.3** THE System SHALL let Administrators add plans, change their prices and retire them. Prices are not fixed in the platform. Existing subscribers SHALL keep their current price until an Administrator moves them.

## 3. Free trials

**SUB-2.1** THE System SHALL let staff give a verified organisation a free trial: staff choose one of the existing plans for the organisation's type and the trial's end date.

**SUB-2.2** WHILE a trial runs, the organisation SHALL have an active subscription with every gated feature (section 4). No payment method is needed and no payment is taken.

**SUB-2.3** WHEN a trial ends, THE System SHALL end it without charging. The organisation then has no active subscription until a manager buys a plan.

**SUB-2.4** THE System SHALL email the organisation's managers 7 days and 1 day before a trial ends, with a link to the plans.

**SUB-2.5** WHILE a trial runs, THE System SHALL let a manager buy a plan, with or without a discount code. The paid plan SHALL start when the trial ends, so no trial time is lost.

**SUB-2.6** Staff MAY give the same organisation more than one free trial. Each trial is recorded in the audit log.

## 4. What a subscription unlocks

**SUB-3.1** An active subscription (paid or trial) SHALL be required for:
1. opening the players directory and player profiles;
2. creating, editing, closing and deleting offers;
3. viewing applications to the organisation's offers and changing their status.

**SUB-3.2** Everything else SHALL be available without a subscription, including: signing in, editing the organisation and own profile, the public organisation page, organisation and offer directories, and messaging and the inbox.

**SUB-3.3** Players and staff SHALL never need a subscription.

**SUB-3.4** THE same rules SHALL apply to every organisation type, brands included.

**SUB-3.5** WHERE an organisation has several managers, THE subscription SHALL apply to all of them.

**SUB-3.6** WHEN a manager without an active subscription opens a gated feature, THE System SHALL show "You need to be subscribed" with a link to the plans.

## 5. Buying a subscription

**SUB-4.1** Only a verified manager of a Published organisation SHALL be able to buy a plan. Managers of Pending, Rejected or Unpublished organisations see the plans but cannot buy.

**SUB-4.2** WHEN a manager chooses a plan, THE System SHALL take them to a secure payment page, collect the payment method and billing details (name, company, address, country, email, phone), and return them to Offload afterwards.

**SUB-4.3** WHEN the first payment succeeds, THE System SHALL activate the subscription at once, show a confirmation page and email a receipt.

**SUB-4.4** IF the payment fails, THEN THE System SHALL show the reason, leave the organisation unsubscribed and let the manager try again.

**SUB-4.5** THE System SHALL prevent an organisation from holding two active subscriptions.

**SUB-4.6** Invoices and receipts SHALL show no VAT and no VAT number field, while Offload is not VAT-registered.

## 6. Discount codes

**SUB-5.1** THE payment page SHALL accept a discount code.

**SUB-5.2** A discount code SHALL have: code (not case-sensitive), percentage off (1 to 100), optional plans it applies to, optional expiry date, optional maximum number of uses, and how long the discount lasts (first payment, a number of months, or for ever).

**SUB-5.3** IF a code is unknown, expired, used up or not valid for the chosen plan, THEN THE System SHALL say so and keep the full price.

**SUB-5.4** WHEN a 100% code is used, THE System SHALL still collect a payment method. WHEN the discount ends, the subscription SHALL renew at the full price.

## 7. Renewals, failures, cancellation and pauses

**SUB-6.1** WHEN a renewal payment succeeds, THE System SHALL extend the subscription and email a receipt.

**SUB-6.2** WHEN a renewal payment fails, THE System SHALL email the managers at once, retry automatically over the following days, and keep access for a grace period of 7 days.

**SUB-6.3** WHEN the grace period ends without payment, THE System SHALL stop access to the gated features, show a banner with an "Update payment method" link, and email the managers.

**SUB-6.4** WHEN payment is recovered, THE System SHALL restore access at once and email a confirmation.

**SUB-6.5** THE System SHALL let a manager cancel the subscription. Access SHALL continue until the end of the period already paid.

**SUB-6.6** THE System SHALL let a manager undo a cancellation before the period ends, and subscribe again after it ends.

**SUB-6.7** THE System SHALL let a manager switch between the monthly and the annual plan of their organisation's type. The switch SHALL take effect when the current period ends; until then the current plan continues unchanged. WHEN the manager cancels before the switch takes effect, the switch SHALL be dropped.

**SUB-6.8** WHILE an organisation is Unpublished by staff, THE System SHALL pause its subscription: no payments are taken and the gated features are not available. WHEN staff republish the organisation, THE System SHALL resume it with the paid time that was left when it was paused (`05` ADM-8.6).

**SUB-6.9** WHEN an annual subscription is due to renew, THE System SHALL email the managers a reminder 14 days, 7 days and 1 day before the renewal date. Monthly subscriptions get no renewal reminders; a failed monthly renewal is notified as in SUB-6.2.

**SUB-6.10** WHEN a subscription has ended (cancelled, or access stopped for non-payment), THE System SHALL email the managers a reminder to subscribe again 1, 2, 7, 30 and 180 days after it ended. THE reminders SHALL stop as soon as the organisation subscribes again.

## 8. Manager's subscription page

**SUB-7.1** THE subscription page SHALL show: plan, status (active, trial, cancelled, past due, paused, ended), price and billing period, start date, who bought it, payment method (card brand and last 4 digits only), next renewal date or trial end date, a plan switch waiting for the next period, billing details and a list of invoices that can be downloaded as PDF.

**SUB-7.2** THE page SHALL let the manager update the payment method, update billing details, switch plan (SUB-6.7), cancel and undo a cancellation, without contacting Offload.

**SUB-7.3** WHEN there is no subscription, THE page SHALL show "Not currently subscribed" and the plans available.

## 9. Staff billing tools

Offload Admins and Administrators can both use these tools (`05` ADM-10.1).

**SUB-8.1** THE dashboard SHALL list subscriptions with organisation, plan, status, price, start date and next payment date or end date, filterable by status, plan, organisation type, start date and end date.

**SUB-8.2** THE System SHALL let staff: give a free trial (section 3), cancel a subscription (at period end or immediately), extend a period, change a plan, apply a discount, and refund a payment in full or in part.

**SUB-8.3** Refunds SHALL be decided by staff case by case. Staff choose the amount, for example the unused months of an annual plan, and the System refunds that amount to the original payment method.

**SUB-8.4** WHEN a payment is fully refunded, THE System SHALL ask staff whether to cancel the subscription immediately.

**SUB-8.8** Staff SHALL cancel an organisation's subscription, including a paused one, before deleting the organisation (`05` ADM-8.7). THE System SHALL refuse to delete an organisation that still has a subscription that is not cancelled.

**SUB-8.5** THE System SHALL show each organisation's payment history, including failed payments and refunds.

**SUB-8.6** THE System SHALL let staff manage discount codes (create, change limits, expire).

**SUB-8.7** THE dashboard SHALL show billing statistics (see `05` ADM-5.1).

## 10. Notifications

**SUB-9.1** THE System SHALL email managers when: a subscription or trial starts, a payment succeeds (receipt), a trial is about to end (SUB-2.4), an annual renewal is near (SUB-6.9), a payment fails, a payment is recovered, access is stopped for non-payment, a subscription is cancelled, a subscription is paused or resumed, a subscription or trial ends, a reminder to subscribe again is due (SUB-6.10), a refund is made, the plan changes.

**SUB-9.2** THE System SHALL notify staff of failed payments and refunds.

## 11. Legal and compliance

**SUB-10.1** THE payment page SHALL link to the Terms and Conditions and the Refund Policy, and SHALL state the price, billing period and that the plan renews automatically until cancelled.

**SUB-10.2** THE System SHALL keep billing records for 6 years, also after an account or organisation is deleted.

**SUB-10.3** THE Refund Policy page SHALL say that refunds, including for unused time after cancellation, are decided case by case by Offload.
