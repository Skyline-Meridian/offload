# 05. Admin Section (Dashboard) Requirements

The admin section is the signed-in back office of the platform, called the **dashboard**. Every signed-in user has one. What it contains depends on the user's role:

| Role | Dashboard sections |
|---|---|
| Player | Profile, verification, inbox, applications, notifications, account settings |
| Manager | Profile, verification, organisation, inbox (switch between personal and organisation), offers, applications, subscription, notifications, account settings |
| Offload Admin | Statistics, verification, users, organisations, offer moderation, subscriptions and billing, reference data, audit log, personal inbox, notifications, account settings |
| Administrator | Everything an Offload Admin has, plus staff accounts and platform settings |

The public website (home, directories, organisation pages, offers, player profiles) is described in `01` to `04`. This document describes the dashboard. Rules already defined elsewhere are referenced, not repeated.

---

## 1. Common to every dashboard

**ADM-1.1** THE System SHALL show the dashboard only to signed-in users, with a menu containing only the sections of the user's role.

**ADM-1.2** THE dashboard SHALL open on the user's profile summary for players and managers, and on the statistics page for staff. WHILE anything is missing for a player's or manager's verification, THE dashboard SHALL show the verification status at the top (`01` GEN-3.6, GEN-5.3).

**ADM-1.3** THE dashboard SHALL show the user's notifications with an unread count, and let the user mark them as read (`01` GEN-7.2, GEN-7.3).

**ADM-1.4** THE dashboard SHALL provide account settings for every user: change email, change password, sign out of all devices (`01` GEN-4.7), newsletter opt-in or opt-out (`01` GEN-7.5), download my data and delete my account (`01` GEN-9.2).

**ADM-1.5** WHEN a user changes their email, THE System SHALL send a verification link to the new address. Until the new address is verified, the old address stays the account's email, and the user keeps every right that depends on a verified email (`01` GEN-3.7).

**ADM-1.6** WHEN a section needs a condition the user does not meet (verification, an organisation, a subscription), THE System SHALL show the section with a message saying what is missing and how to get it, instead of hiding it.

## 3. Manager dashboard

| Section | Contents | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | View and edit the manager profile | `03` section 4 |
| Verification | Email status; identity document upload; review status and rejection reason | `01` section 7 |
| Organisation | WHEN the manager has no organisation: "Add organisation" or "Claim an existing organisation". Otherwise: view and edit the organisation, see "Changes awaiting review", see the organisation's status, and remove the organisation from the profile | `03` sections 2 to 7, ORG-1.6, ORG-1.8 |
| Inbox | One inbox with a switch between **Personal** (the manager's own conversation threads) and **Organisation** (threads addressed to the organisation, shared by all its managers; replies show the manager's name). Each side shows its own unread count. | `04` MSG-1.4 to MSG-1.6 |
| Offers | All the organisation's offers (Published, Closed, Expired) with status, expiry date and number of applications; create, edit, close, copy as new, delete | `04` section 1, OFF-1.6 |
| Applications | Applications to the organisation's offers; filter by offer and status; change status | `04` section 2 |
| Subscription | Plan, status, renewal date, payment method, billing details, invoices; subscribe, change payment method, cancel | `06` |

**ADM-3.1** THE Offers, Applications and Subscription sections SHALL appear only WHILE the manager is verified and approved for an organisation (`03` ORG-4.2). Offers and Applications SHALL show an upgrade message WHILE the organisation has no active subscription (`03` ORG-10.1).

**ADM-3.2** THE Organisation section SHALL show the names of the organisation's other managers. A manager cannot add, remove or approve other managers (`03` ORG-1.5).

**ADM-3.3** WHILE an organisation is Pending or Rejected, THE System SHALL let the manager who registered it edit it, even before the manager is verified. Changes apply at once, because the organisation is not public. Once the organisation is Published, only verified and approved managers can edit it (`03` ORG-7.1).

**ADM-3.4** WHEN staff reject an organisation the manager registered, THE System SHALL let the manager edit it and send it for review again. It then returns to Pending.

**ADM-3.5** WHEN staff reject a manager, THE System SHALL let the manager upload a new identity document and, for a manager who claimed an existing organisation, also choose a different organisation or register a new one.

## 4. Staff dashboard

Staff are Offload Admins and Administrators.

### 4.1 Staff roles and accounts

| Role | Who | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Offload Admin | Offload employees | Statistics, verification, users (including deleting accounts), organisations, offer moderation, subscriptions and billing (including refunds), reference data, audit log |
| Administrator | Super admins, for example the developers | Everything an Offload Admin can, plus creating and removing staff accounts, changing roles, platform settings and plans |

**ADM-4.1** THE System SHALL allow only Offload Admins and Administrators into the staff sections.

**ADM-4.2** Only Administrators SHALL create staff accounts, change roles and change platform settings. A role change SHALL only make a user staff (Offload Admin or Administrator) or return a staff member to their previous role; players and managers cannot be changed into each other. Staff accounts are not created through public registration.

**ADM-4.3** THE System SHALL always have at least one Administrator. The last Administrator SHALL not be removed, suspended, deleted or changed to another role.

**ADM-4.4** WHEN a new staff member accepts the invitation from an Administrator, THE System SHALL treat their email as verified. Staff accounts SHALL not be deleted as unverified (`01` GEN-3.10) and do not need document verification.

**ADM-4.5** Staff accounts SHALL sign in with email and password only. Two-factor sign-in is not required.

**ADM-4.6** Staff SHALL not create, edit or delete offers, SHALL not change applications, and SHALL not read, change or delete other users' messages. Apart from offer moderation (section 4.6), staff see only counts of offers, applications and messages (section 4.2) and the audit log (section 4.9).

**ADM-4.7** Staff SHALL not sign in as another user or view the platform as another user. To reproduce a problem, staff use their own test accounts of the role concerned.

**ADM-4.8** Staff are users of the platform and SHALL have a personal inbox, with the same conversation threads as other users (`04` MSG-1.6). Staff can message any user from it, or contact users by email outside the platform.

**ADM-4.9** Staff SHALL not appear in any directory and SHALL have no public profile, so users normally reach them only by replying to a conversation that staff started. The System SHALL not block a user from starting a conversation with a staff member.

**ADM-4.10** WHEN something new needs a staff action (a player, manager or organisation waiting for review, or a change awaiting review), THE System SHALL notify every staff member by email and in the app. THE System SHALL also email every staff member a daily summary of everything still waiting, and show the number waiting next to each list in the menu.

### 4.2 Statistics

**ADM-5.1** THE statistics page SHALL show:

| Metric |
|---|
| Total players |
| Players with email verified / not verified |
| Players with document verified / not verified |
| Players awaiting review |
| Clubs, unions, colleges/universities and brands: published / pending, per type |
| Managers awaiting review, per organisation type |
| Active offers, expired offers |
| Total applications |
| Total messages (count only) |
| New registrations per week, per role |
| Active subscriptions per plan |
| Monthly recurring revenue |
| Failed payments in the last 30 days |
| Subscriptions renewing in the next 30 days |

**ADM-5.2** Each user and organisation metric SHALL link to the matching filtered list.

### 4.3 Verification

Managers and organisations are verified in separate places, as on the current site.

**ADM-6.1** THE System SHALL provide these verification lists:

| List | Tabs | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| Verify users | Players, Club managers, Union managers, College / University managers, Brand managers | Name, email, organisation (for managers: the organisation they registered or claimed, and whether it is new or existing), date joined, email verified, document, time waiting, actions |
| Verify organisations | Clubs, Unions, Colleges / Universities, Brands | Logo, name, address, country, date created, document, possible duplicate, time waiting, actions |
| Changes awaiting review | One list for all types | Organisation, changed fields with old and new values, requested by, date, actions |

**ADM-6.2** Each list SHALL be searchable, sortable and paged, oldest first by default. An empty list shows "Nothing to review".

**ADM-6.3** THE System SHALL let staff open identity and organisation documents inside the dashboard without making them public (`01` GEN-8.3).

**ADM-6.4** WHEN staff approve a player or a manager, THE System SHALL mark the document verified, record who and when, and notify the user. For a manager, approval also makes the link to the organisation active (`03` ORG-6.2).

**ADM-6.5** WHEN staff approve an organisation, THE System SHALL publish it and notify its managers. Approving an organisation SHALL not approve its managers; each manager is approved in "Verify users".

**ADM-6.6** WHEN staff approve a change awaiting review, THE System SHALL apply the new values (`03` ORG-7.3).

**ADM-6.7** WHEN staff reject a user, an organisation or a change, THE System SHALL require a reason and send it to the user or the organisation's managers. What the manager can do next is in ADM-3.4 and ADM-3.5.

**ADM-6.8** THE System SHALL let staff revoke a verification. A revoked player leaves the players directory. A revoked manager loses rights over the organisation. Revoking an organisation is the same action as unpublishing it (ADM-8.5). Staff SHALL not revoke the last verified manager of an organisation (`03` ORG-1.8).

**ADM-6.9** THE System SHALL let staff approve several selected items at once after a confirmation. Rejections are made one at a time, each with its own reason.

### 4.4 Users

**ADM-7.1** THE System SHALL let staff search and filter users by role, organisation type, verification state, date joined, country and status (active, suspended).

**ADM-7.2** THE System SHALL let staff view a user's account and profile, edit profile fields (including email, name and date of birth), send a password reset link, suspend and reactivate an account, and delete an account. Changes made by staff SHALL not send a verified user back to review, and SHALL be recorded in the audit log with old and new values.

**ADM-7.3** WHILE an account is suspended:
- the user SHALL not be able to sign in;
- a suspended player SHALL not appear in the players directory;
- other users SHALL still see past messages with the user, but SHALL not be able to send them new messages;
- the user's open applications SHALL be withdrawn.

**ADM-7.4** Staff SHALL not suspend, delete or revoke the last verified manager of an organisation (`03` ORG-1.8).

**ADM-7.5** WHEN a player's account is deleted, by the player or by staff, THE System SHALL withdraw their applications and show the player as "Deleted user" in them, as in messages (`04` MSG-1.12).

**ADM-7.6** THE System SHALL let staff export a filtered user list (name, role, organisation type, email, verification state, newsletter opt-in) to a spreadsheet file. Each export SHALL be recorded in the audit log.

### 4.5 Organisations

**ADM-8.1** THE System SHALL let staff search and filter organisations by type, status, country and subscription status.

**ADM-8.2** THE System SHALL let staff view and edit every field of any organisation, whatever its status, and see its managers, its subscription and the number of its offers and applications. A change made by staff SHALL apply at once, without the review in `03` ORG-7.2, and SHALL be recorded in the audit log with old and new values.

**ADM-8.3** THE System SHALL let staff remove a manager from an organisation. Staff SHALL not remove the last verified manager (`03` ORG-1.8). Staff SHALL not add a manager directly; managers join by registering or claiming, and staff approve them (`03` ORG-1.5).

**ADM-8.4** WHEN the same organisation has been registered twice, THE System SHALL let staff keep one record and unpublish the other (ADM-8.5), and cancel and refund the other record's subscription with the billing tools (`06`). Records are not merged.

**ADM-8.5** THE System SHALL let staff unpublish an organisation, for example when it breaks Offload's terms, and republish it later. Unpublishing sets the organisation's status to Unpublished (`03` ORG-1.1). Staff SHALL give a reason, which is sent to the organisation's managers.

**ADM-8.6** WHILE an organisation is Unpublished, THE System SHALL hide its public page, remove it from directories and hide its offers from players. Its managers can still sign in and see their dashboard, with a notice showing the reason. Its subscription SHALL be paused: no payments are taken and the gated features are not available. WHEN staff republish the organisation, THE System SHALL resume the subscription with the paid time that was left when it was paused.

**ADM-8.7** Staff SHALL remove an organisation from the platform by unpublishing it. Staff SHALL delete an organisation only WHILE it is Unpublished and has no offers and no applications. WHEN staff delete an organisation:
- its managers lose the link to it and are asked to choose or register an organisation (`03` ORG-4.2);
- its conversations are kept, with the organisation shown as "Deleted organisation", as for deleted users (`04` MSG-1.12);
- its billing records are kept (`06` SUB-9.2).

**ADM-8.8** Staff SHALL not change an organisation's type in the first release. WHEN an organisation was registered with the wrong type, its manager registers it again with the right type, and staff unpublish the wrong record.

### 4.6 Offer moderation

**ADM-9.1** THE System SHALL let staff see an offer's public details and close it WHEN it breaks Offload's terms. Staff SHALL give a reason, which is sent to the organisation's managers.

**ADM-9.2** WHEN staff close an offer, THE System SHALL treat it like any closed offer (`04` OFF-1.11): it leaves the offers directory and stops new applications. The managers' offers section SHALL show "Closed by Offload" with the reason, and "Copy as new offer" (`04` OFF-1.13) SHALL not be available for it.

**ADM-9.3** Staff SHALL not reopen, edit or delete an offer, and SHALL not see or change its applications.

### 4.7 Subscriptions and billing

**ADM-10.1** Staff billing tools are defined in `06` section 8. Offload Admins and Administrators can both use them.

### 4.8 Reference data

**ADM-11.1** THE System SHALL let staff add, rename, reorder and retire entries in: positions, soft skills, game skills, qualifications (`02` section 9), leagues (`03` section 5) and industries (`03` section 3.5). Retiring an entry hides it from forms and filters but keeps it on existing profiles.

### 4.9 Audit log

The audit log is the staff's source of information for supporting users.

**ADM-12.1** THE System SHALL record in the audit log:

| Group | Events |
|---|---|
| Account | Registration, email verified, sign-in, failed sign-in, lockout, password reset, sign out of all devices, email change, suspended, reactivated, account deleted |
| Verification | Document uploaded, approved, rejected (with reason), revoked |
| Organisation | Created, claimed, edited (changed fields, by manager or staff), change submitted and decided, published, unpublished (with reason), republished, merged, manager joined, removed or left |
| Offers and applications | Offer published, edited, closed (by a manager, or by staff with reason), copied, deleted, expired; application submitted, status changed, withdrawn |
| Messaging | Message sent: sender, recipient and time only, never the content |
| Billing | Subscription started, renewed, paused, resumed, payment failed, recovered, cancelled, ended, refund, plan change |
| Staff | Every staff action, with old and new values |

**ADM-12.2** THE audit log SHALL never contain message content, passwords, document contents or payment card details.

**ADM-12.3** THE System SHALL let staff search the audit log by user, organisation, event type and date range, and SHALL show a timeline on each user's and organisation's staff page.

**ADM-12.4** THE audit log SHALL not be editable or deletable through the dashboard.

**ADM-12.5** THE System SHALL keep audit log events for 2 years, and billing and subscription events for 6 years (the same period as billing records, `06` SUB-9.2), then delete them automatically.

**ADM-12.6** WHEN a user's account is deleted, THE System SHALL keep their audit events with the user's id only, and SHALL remove their name and email from them. The audit log then shows the user as "Deleted user".

### 4.10 Platform settings and support (Administrators)

**ADM-13.1** THE System SHALL let Administrators set: email sender name and address, support email address, and the current terms version.

**ADM-13.2** Users accept the terms at registration and cannot register without accepting (`01` GEN-2.2). WHEN the terms version changes, THE System SHALL show every user a notification asking them to accept the new terms, until they accept. Not accepting SHALL not restrict any use of the platform.

**ADM-13.3** Website content (home page, about, pricing text, legal pages, sponsors, testimonials, league table) SHALL be changed by Administrators as part of development, not through the dashboard.

**ADM-13.4** WHEN a visitor or user sends the contact form, THE System SHALL send it by email to the support email address (ADM-13.1). Contact form messages are not stored in the dashboard.
